# Planned Features

Approved or accepted future features.

## Planned

- Feature: Replace all placeholder contact info with real business details (WhatsApp number, full address, phone, email).
  - Reason: Critical for real lead generation.
  - Priority: High
  - Notes: Update in both index.html and form.html + project-docs.

- Feature: Add a photo gallery or "Real batteries in the Experience Center" section.
  - Reason: Move from pure stylized visuals to showing actual product/experience.
  - Priority: Medium
  - Notes: Client may provide images.

- Feature: Improve mobile experience for the Experience Center (touch interactions, responsive layer UI).
  - Reason: Many visitors will be on phones.
  - Priority: Medium-High

- Feature: Add subtle background particle/energy effects or canvas animations in hero and Experience Center.
  - Reason: Increase the "futuristic" immersion.
  - Priority: Low-Medium

## Ideas Accepted For Later

- Idea: Migrate from CDN Tailwind/GSAP to a proper Vite + Tailwind + GSAP build setup.
  - Why later: Current approach is working extremely well for speed and editability. Only needed if site grows significantly.
  - Notes: Keep as an option.

- Idea: Real backend for lead storage (e.g. simple Express + Google Sheets or Airtable, or Formspree + WhatsApp API).
  - Why later: Adds complexity and cost. Current client-side solution is sufficient for launch.

- Idea: Add booking calendar integration (Calendly or similar) from the form or "Book Experience" buttons.
  - Why later: Nice to have after basic lead flow is proven.
